PS 1

Smart Environmental Monitoring and Protection

Design and develop an innovative hardware-based solution to monitor, analyse, or mitigate environmental challenges. The solution may address air or water quality, pollution, waste management, noise pollution, resource conservation, ecosystem monitoring, or other environmental concerns.

The system should demonstrate how hardware, sensing technologies, intelligent analysis, or automated mechanisms can help detect environmental problems, provide actionable insights, or enable timely intervention.

PS 2

Intelligent Industrial Systems and Automation

Develop a hardware-based solution that improves the efficiency, reliability, safety, or intelligence of industrial and infrastructure systems. Potential applications include equipment monitoring, predictive maintenance, process automation, energy management, fault detection, industrial data acquisition, and intelligent control.

The solution should demonstrate a practical engineering approach to solving an operational challenge, with an emphasis on reliability, affordability, adaptability, and real-world deployment.

PS 3

Smart Safety, Hazard Detection and Risk Prevention

Build an innovative hardware-based system that identifies potential hazards, monitors dangerous conditions, or helps prevent accidents in workplaces, public spaces, transportation systems, or other high-risk environments.

Applications may include mine safety, fire and gas hazard detection, electrical safety, structural risk monitoring, industrial worker protection, confined-space safety, and early warning systems. Solutions may use sensors, embedded intelligence, robotics, communication networks, or automated response mechanisms.

PS 4

Intelligent Healthcare, Assistive Technology and Human Safety

Design and prototype a hardware-based solution that improves human safety, health monitoring, accessibility, independent living, or emergency assistance.

Possible applications include assistive devices for people with disabilities, elderly care systems, wearable safety devices, remote physiological monitoring, emergency alert systems, rehabilitation aids, and intelligent personal assistance. Solutions should prioritise usability, affordability, reliability, and the needs of their intended users.

PS 5

Smart Infrastructure, Disaster Management and Resilient Communities

Develop a hardware-based solution that addresses challenges in public infrastructure, urban development, transportation, agriculture, water management, disaster preparedness, or emergency response.

Potential applications include flood and landslide monitoring, rockfall prediction, infrastructure inspection, intelligent traffic systems, precision agriculture, water leakage detection, search-and-rescue robotics, and resource optimisation. The solution should demonstrate how physical systems and intelligent technologies can improve situational awareness, reduce risks, or support faster and better-informed decisions.
