# HackerRank partner assets

The landing page's coding partner panel uses the supplied HackerRank brand guide, including the black (`#141419`), Hacker Green (`#05C770`), white, and Celery (`#DBFFC2`) palette, Satoshi regular and bold, and default letter spacing. The official logo is displayed unmodified and at its original aspect ratio, with space around it.

- Brand guide supplied by the organizer: `hackerrank-brand-guides.pdf`.
- Official logo folder linked from page 2: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1qphyNjAmc7kzuXTkwB1CxbZpQC14Mwlu
- Logo: `Logo Light Wide.svg`, Drive file ID `1_AKzCajz1qSy9WBlwQCKqlM14z2o9vIZ`, saved as `public/images/partners/hackerrank-logo-light.svg`.
- Satoshi regular and bold: Fontshare's Satoshi webfont distribution, https://api.fontshare.com/v2/css?f[]=satoshi@400,700&display=swap, saved under `public/fonts/`. Font information and licensing: https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/satoshi and https://www.fontshare.com/licenses/itf-ffl.

The hardware event links to the organizer's new rulebook. `public/hardware-problem-statements.html` transcribes PS 1–5 from the three supplied images, preserving their wording and numbering, with direct links, responsive text, and a print layout.
